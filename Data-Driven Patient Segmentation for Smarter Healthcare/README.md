Patient Segmentation for Smarter Healthcare

The Problem We Addressed
In todays healthcare system, hospitals face critical challenges, such as rising treatment costs, inefficient resource allocation, and diverse patient needs. Addressing these issues requires actionable insights drawn from vast amounts of patient data.  

Our project aimed to solve this real-world problem by analyzing hospital data using Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and Clustering. The goal was to provide hospitals with tools to offer personalized care, optimize resources, and enhance the overall patient experience.

Our Approach :
1. Simplifying Complex Data  
   Using PCA, we reduced the complexity of a multi-dimensional dataset. This step retained essential details while making the data easier to interpret.  

2. Identifying Patient Groups  
   Applying K-means clustering, we divided patients into 8 meaningful groups based on shared traits, such as:  
   - Patients with chronic conditions requiring ongoing care.  
   - Young, preventive health-focused individuals.  
   These clusters revealed distinct patterns that hospitals could act upon to tailor their services and allocate resources effectively.  

3. Validation for Reliability
   To ensure accuracy and reliability, we evaluated the clustering results using:  
   - Silhouette Score for cohesion within clusters.  
   - Calinski-Harabasz Index to measure cluster separation.  
   - Davies-Bouldin Index to minimize overlaps and improve clarity.  

Real-World Impact   
This project turns complex data into insights, revolutionizing healthcare delivery.:  
- Personalized Treatment Plans: Tailoring care to individual needs based on cluster insights.  
- Efficient Resource Management: Optimizing hospital operations to serve diverse patient groups.  
- Cost Reduction: Minimizing unnecessary expenses through better decision-making.

This work shows how data-driven strategies can help hospitals improve patient outcomes and reduce costs, creating smarter and more efficient healthcare systems.
